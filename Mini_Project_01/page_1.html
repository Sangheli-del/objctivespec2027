This is the first page of the TF Wiki

The Transformers, Robots in Disguise, as you all know which started with the classic "Autobots wage their battle to destroy the evil forces of the Decepticons" which eventually became so much more through more shows after Generation One. Such as Beast Wars, comics, more animated reboots, games, Aligned Continuity, and all the movies.

As such, one would expect many, many continuities across the many mediums out of the series: the cartoons first and foremost, through most would probably be far more comfortable with Michael Bay's Transformers movies which might have been a good introduction to most kids in the 2000s and 2010s like me.
